# Operation-Meow
A 2D thriller puzzle platformer made in Unity for Pixel Forge Jam 2026.

**Play on Itch:** [Operation Meow](https://jesterstudio.itch.io/operation-meow)

## Story
Kidnapped, imprisoned and forced to be a cat, Jason is trapped in a bizarre facility, where strict compliance is the only option. Forced to abide by their rigid rules with cats constantly monitoring his every move, he must rely on his new feline form to survive. But to finally escape, he will have to shatter the very rules designed to keep him contained.

## Core Mechanics
* **Rule Breaking Gameplay:** Built around jam theme "Rules are meant to be broken", the game actively defies traditional platforming logic. The interface itself acts as a physical puzzle piece.
* **UI Interaction:** Players must physically alter the environment to progress, such as swatting away restriction signs to unlock abilities like double jumping.
* **Reclaiming Humanity:** Text elements and UI components get ripped directly from the screen to solve puzzles and navigate the levels.

## Controls
**Keyboard:**
* **Move:** `A`/ `D` or `Arrow keys`
* **Jump:** `Spacebar` or `Up Arrow`
* **Stand/Crawl:** `Shift` or `Down Arrow`
* **Interact:** `E`
* **Pause:** `Escape`

**PlayStation Controller**
* **Move:** `Left Stick`
* **Jump:** `Cross(X)`
* **Stand/Crawl:** `R2`
* **Interact:** `Square`
* **Pause:** `Options`
